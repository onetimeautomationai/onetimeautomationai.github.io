AI Lead Capture Machine

Sharing what we are doing in our real estate business. One listing showed me how I actually scale it.

Leo Chen · 3 min read · Build the system first

I run 2 businesses and have for years. One is an AI agency where I help business owners build systems and automations. The other is a real estate sales business where I transact as an agent. Last month, one listing in my real estate business showed me something about how I actually scale it.

Traditionally business owners think the next step after a good year is to hire. A coordinator, an assistant, more people to carry more volume. In my business, the next step was to build a system first.

For years I believed scaling meant headcount too. Transaction coordinator, assistant, in-house admin, more agents to close more deals. That is what I used to tell people. But in the AI age, I stopped believing more volume meant more hires.

Here is what changed my mind, and what I would tell any business owner starting this year. I call it building the system first.

The $1.65 million listing

A $1.65 million listing is the clearest example. Six agents called about it. Four leads came in from Zillow. Two private showings went to buyers who were not represented yet. 31 groups came through the open house in a single day. Every one of them got logged automatically and followed up within an hour, after we had captured them, spoken with them, and they had left the open house. Not because I had to remember it or write it down on a piece of paper, or worse, whenever I got around to it. Three offers came in within 10 days.

None of that happened because I hired someone to watch it. It happened because the system watches it.

What that looks like in practice

It is not real estate specific:

  1. Every lead gets a thank you email and text, with the listing link and details, 1 hour after they sign in.
  2. Every call, text, and email gets logged, captured, and followed up the same day.
  3. Customer conversations, preferences, and history are saved automatically, so I am never asking the same question twice.
  4. All lead activities are logged, so I always know where things stand without checking 10 different places.
If your business depends on you personally remembering a hundred small details, that dependency is the real bottleneck, not your volume.

And hiring another person to remember it all for you is not a solution.
