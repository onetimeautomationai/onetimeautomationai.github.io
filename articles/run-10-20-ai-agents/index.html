How I Run 10-20 AI Agents at the Same Time

I use one screen and one tool, and I no longer wait on one agent at a time.

Leo Chen · 1 min read

I run anywhere from 10-20 AI agents at the same time on one screen regularly.

What Herdr gives me

I use a tool called Herdr, and it gives me three benefits:

1. I stop waiting on one agent at a time. I hand work to several agents at once, and they report back to me when they finish. That makes me the orchestrator, and my agents do the work in parallel.

2. Everything stays organized in one window. Spaces hold tabs, tabs hold panes, and every pane is one agent. The pane I work in highlights in orange, so I always know which agent I am talking to. My setup has six workspaces and about 30 tabs.

3. Nothing gets lost. If I close the window or reboot my computer, Herdr takes me right back to where I left off.
