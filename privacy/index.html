Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This policy explains what information One Time Automation AI ("we", "us") collects when you visit this site, buy The 5-Agent CEO System, or contact support, and what we do with it. If anything is unclear, email leo@onetimeautomation.ai.

Information we collect

  • Purchase details. When you buy, Stripe collects your name, email address, and payment details on its secure checkout form. We never see or store your full card number. We receive your name, email address, and order information from Stripe.
  • Support messages. Support is for customers. If you use the support form, we receive the name, email address, and message you type, and we check your email against our purchase records to send you a one-time confirmation code. The code and your message are kept only briefly while we confirm it is you.
  • Order and download records. To deliver your purchase we keep your order number, a count of downloads for that order, and a record that your thank-you email was sent.
  • Basic usage data. Our pages use Google Tag Manager, which can collect standard information such as pages visited, device and browser type, and approximate location.

How we use it

  • To process your payment and deliver your download.
  • To email you a thank-you message with your copy of the product.
  • To answer your support questions and requests.
  • To keep the site working, prevent abuse and fraud, and understand how the site is used.
  • To meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising by third parties.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the services that help us run the site and your order:

  • Stripe for payments and receipts.
  • Google Workspace (Gmail) for sending your thank-you email and receiving support messages.
  • Netlify for the secure download and form services.
  • GitHub Pages for hosting this website.
  • Google Tag Manager for site analytics.

Each of these providers handles data under its own privacy policy. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Cookies and analytics

This site uses Google Tag Manager and the cookies or similar technologies it sets to measure traffic. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and the site will still work. Stripe may also use cookies on its checkout form to process payments securely and prevent fraud.

How long we keep it

We keep order and payment records for as long as needed for tax, accounting, and legal reasons. We keep support messages as long as needed to help you and to keep a record of the conversation. Download-count records are kept with the order. You can ask us to delete your information at any time, subject to records we must keep by law.

Security

Downloads are protected by short-lived links that expire after 15 minutes, and each order is limited to 3 downloads. Payments are handled by Stripe. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act. To make a request, email leo@onetimeautomation.ai from the address you used when you bought or contacted us. We may need to confirm it is you before we act on a request.

Children

This site is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed. Using the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information? Email leo@onetimeautomation.ai or use the support form.
