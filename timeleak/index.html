The Time-Leak Checklist

Ten spots where your day gets taken from you a few minutes at a time, and the one thing you hand an AI agent to shut each one.

A time thief works the same spots every day, and it counts on you never adding them up. Read down the list and mark the ones that are yours.

Under each leak is the fix: the one job you hand an agent so it stops happening. You are not asking a chatbot for a one-off answer. You are training something to run that job for you, every day, on its own.

01

The follow-up you log by hand

You finish a call with a client, then open your system and type out what just happened in your own words. Once, it is nothing. Forty times a week, it is a workday.

Hand it off: have an agent listen to the call and write the note for you. You read it. You don't write it.
02

The copy and paste between tools

The same name and number goes into your CRM, then your calendar, then a text thread, retyped each time and checked each time so you don't fat-finger it.

Hand it off: let one entry write to every place it needs to land, so you type it once and it shows up everywhere.
03

The reply you've typed a hundred times

Someone asks the question you know cold, pricing or timelines or how something works, and you type the answer from scratch because that feels faster than finding it.

Hand it off: keep your best answer ready to fire back, written once and reused, instead of rebuilt every time someone asks.
04

The write-up you start from a blank page

Every new job, you sit down to the empty box and build the write-up from zero, in your voice, matching the last one you were happy with.

Hand it off: give an agent the facts and three words for your tone, and let it hand you a draft to edit instead of a blank page to fill.
05

The scramble before every call

Ten minutes before a client call you dig through emails and notes to remember where you left off, what they wanted, and what you promised.

Hand it off: have an agent pull the history and hand you a one-page brief before the call, so you walk in warm instead of cold.
06

The lead that cooled while it sat

An inquiry comes in while you're away from your desk. By the time you see it and reply, the person has moved on to whoever answered first.

Hand it off: let an agent send the first real reply the moment someone reaches out, so nobody waits on you to be free.
07

The pipeline you check by scrolling

To know where you stand you open the CRM and scroll, hoping you notice the deal that has gone quiet or the step you forgot.

Hand it off: have an agent read the pipeline for you and tell you what's slipping, so you get a heads-up instead of a scroll.
08

The idea you had in the car

A good thought lands between meetings, the perfect line for a client or a post, and it's gone by the time you're back at a desk.

Hand it off: talk it into your phone and let an agent catch it, sort it, and file it where you'll actually find it later.
09

The inbox you triage one by one

Every morning you read down a full inbox deciding what matters, what waits, and what is noise, before a single one of them gets an answer.

Hand it off: let an agent sort the inbox into what needs you today and what can wait, so you start with the short list.
10

The update you rebuild from zero

A report or an update goes out on a schedule, and every time you assemble the numbers and the wording again from scratch.

Hand it off: have an agent pull the current numbers and fill your format, so the update writes itself and you just approve it.

You don't fix ten leaks with ten different tools. You fix them by putting one agent on the clock whose only job, every night, is to walk back through your day and find where the hours went.

Start with the one you marked first. Hand that single job over this week and let it run. Then come back to the list and hand over the next.
