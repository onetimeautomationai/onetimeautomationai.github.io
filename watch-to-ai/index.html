What it does

You record a voice memo on your Apple Watch, and it syncs to your Mac. A scheduled job transcribes the memo locally, then Claude files it, writes down the actions, and does the work. When the job finishes, you get one iMessage report.

What you need: A Mac. An Apple Watch with Voice Memos syncing through iCloud. Claude Code.

Setup steps

Follow these in order. Each step is short.

  1. Install a local transcription tool. The simplest is pip3 install openai-whisper plus brew install ffmpeg. Test it with whisper some-file.m4a --model base.
  2. Copy config.example.json to config.json in the folder root.
  3. In config.json, set notes_folder to the folder where your notes live.
  4. Check transcribe_command. It must print the transcript, or write {stem}.txt into {outdir}. The words {audio} and {outdir} are filled in for each recording.
  5. Open System Settings, Privacy and Security, Full Disk Access. Add your terminal app. Add /usr/bin/python3 too if you plan to use launchd.
  6. Make sure claude -p "say hi" works in your terminal.
  7. Run the script once by hand: python3 scripts/voice_memo_ingest.py. It marks your existing recordings as seen and stops.
  8. Record a short test memo on your watch. Wait for iCloud to sync it to your Mac. Run the script again. Check your notes folder.
  9. Copy setup/com.example.voice-memo-ingest.plist to ~/Library/LaunchAgents/. Replace every REPLACE_WITH_ value.
  10. Load it: launchctl load ~/Library/LaunchAgents/com.example.voice-memo-ingest.plist
  11. Optional: set notify_imessage to true and notify_number to your own number. Messages must be signed in on this Mac.

The inject prompt

Use this when your AI tool cannot read a folder. Paste everything in the box into custom instructions, then paste a transcript as your message.

Paste into custom instructions 
Every message I send you is a voice memo transcript. Treat it as a one-way instruction. I will not answer questions until later, so never ask any. Make your best guess and flag what was unclear.

For each memo: show the full transcript first, unedited. Name the topic and say which folder or note it belongs in. List the actions in short lines. Then do the work the memo asks for, right in your reply, and mark each action done. If the memo says what "done" looks like, treat that as your finish line. If something needs a human, such as a call or a purchase, list it under "Needs you" with the exact next step. Never send anything to other people. Draft it instead. End with one short summary of one or two sentences.
